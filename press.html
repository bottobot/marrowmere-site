Description

Marrowmere is a 16-bit-style story adventure set on closing night in a northwestern Ontario quarry town whose own dead do the scaring. The Clerk is closing everything, and everyone calls it sabotage. Walk the town, talk to the living and the kept, and find what is listening in the stone.

Marrowmere is a township of 712 living people and 86 kept dead on the north shore of a lake in northwestern Ontario, half an hour east of Kenora. It lived on granite for ninety-four years. Since 1976 it has lived on the Show, in which its own dead do the scaring. Its motto is cut over the Gate: IT WILL HOLD.

You are the night attendant on the Show's closing night, Thursday 24 September 2026. The Clerk, Josiah Gumm, is closing the town piece by piece, and everyone calls it sabotage. Walk the street strips, talk with the residents, gather clues and follow them to a masked congregation that worships something in the stone. Four residents can die by your word. There are three endings. One is hidden.

History

Marrowmere began in September 2026 as a story for a small handheld game device. The town, its residents and its history were written first. In October 2026 the game moved to Godot 4 and became a 16-bit-style side-scroller at 480 by 270, so the town can be walked street by street.

Features

  • A town to walk. Walk the street strips from the viaduct to the Quarry Lip and open the doors: more than seventy places, from the Clerk's office to the hotel attic.
  • The living and kept. Talk with thirty principal residents, living and kept. Each wants one stupid thing very badly and keeps one secret. Come back, and they tell you more.
  • A case to build. Gather evidence across the town and build your case: what was done, who did it, and why. Every clue is true. Read them carefully.
  • Choices kill. Four residents can die by your word. Every death is foreshadowed, and every fatal line is offered beside a safe one. Listen to the town before you answer.
  • One night, three endings. Play the Show's closing night, Thursday 24 September 2026, from the first coach to the last tour. Three endings. One of them is hidden.
  • A 16-bit night. A 16-bit-style town at 480 by 270 in indigo, violet and lamplight. The horror is in what you hear and what goes missing. No gore, ever.

AI-generated content

Marrowmere is made with generative AI, and we want players to know how. The backgrounds, the character art and animations, and the new shots in the teaser were generated with Grok and Gemini, then chosen, edited, keyed and assembled by Keepstone Games. The story, the dialogue and the website's text were drafted with AI writing tools and edited by Keepstone Games. The teaser's music and sound are synthesized in code. Nothing is generated by AI while you play.
