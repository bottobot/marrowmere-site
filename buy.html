Questions

When does Marrowmere come out?

In 2027. The release date will be announced on the store pages first. Wishlist Marrowmere on Steam and Steam will tell you the day it comes out. The store pages open before release; until they do, the store buttons on this page say so.

How much does it cost, and where can I buy it?

The Standard Edition is $9.99 USD. The Deluxe Edition is $14.99 USD and adds the original soundtrack and a digital art book. Each store shows its price in your own currency, and regional prices are set when the store pages open. Marrowmere is planned for Steam, itch.io and GOG; this site has no checkout.

Which platforms is it coming to?

Windows, macOS and Linux. Those are the only platforms announced. Steam Deck support is planned.

Will it run on Steam Deck?

Steam Deck support is planned. The game draws at 480x270 and scales by whole numbers, so on the Deck's 1280x800 screen it shows at exact 2x pixels. Valve rates Deck compatibility itself after its own review, so there is no rating to show yet.

What are the controls?

Keyboard and gamepad (Xbox layout) are both planned. You walk and run through the streets, look at what you find and act on it. There is no jumping.

Which languages does it support?

English at launch, for all text and the interface. The dialogue is written, not voiced.

How long is it?

To be announced. The story covers one evening, the Show's closing night, and we will publish an honest playtime once the whole night can be played from start to finish.

Is it scary? What should I know before playing?

It is folk horror written for teen players and up: dread and atmosphere, never gore. Some residents can die because of what you say. The content notes below list everything.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds follow the policy of the store you bought from: Steam, itch.io and GOG each publish their own. You buy Marrowmere on those stores, not on this site, so the store handles any refund.

I write about games or make videos. Can I get a key?

Yes. Keys go out closer to release, and a press contact address is coming soon. Meanwhile, see the press kit for the fact sheet, images and logo.

Content notes

Marrowmere is folk horror for teen players and up. This is what it contains, so you can decide before you play.

Themes
Death, grief, a secretive cult, peril and dread. The town's dead, the kept, live alongside the living, and some of them choose to rest for good.
Deaths
Some residents can die as a result of your choices. Each death happens off screen: you hear it, and then someone is missing. There is no gore and no blood, and no body or injury is ever shown.
The town's past
The town remembers quarry accidents from its history, and the people it lost in them, through its records and memorials.
Flashing
No strobing or rapidly flashing effects are planned. We will test the finished game and update this note before release.
Planned rating
Teen and up, about 13 and over. Final age ratings are set through each store's rating process before release.

Marrowmere's art was generated with AI tools. The press kit explains how the game is made.
